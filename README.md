Entry for the make.fun competition
[Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3):
one sentence, at most 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in
everyday life, judged pairwise by an AI judge that rewards surprise.

## The entry

> At 6 a.m. the lift doors open on a stranger humming the song my
> grandmother used to sing while shelling peas, and for three floors
> the building forgets we are strangers.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
Deterministic — the same bytes on every run, no network, no input.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
